# AERO Task Hub Chief Engineer guide

This guide is for the Chief Engineer. Open it from **Help**, then the **Chief Engineer guide** tab; nobody else sees that tab. The **Member guide** and **Officer guide** apply to you too. This guide adds only what the Chief Engineer alone can do in the website.

Updating, backing up and restoring the website are in the **Administrator guide** tab, with a restart PDF to keep outside the website.

## People

1. Select **People**. Search by name or email, then select **Search**. Each page shows up to 25 people; **Next** and **Previous** keep your search. **Clear** resets it.
2. Select **Add person**, enter the name and Google email, and choose the role and **Class · Subteam** membership. Select **Add person** to save. The person can sign in with Google straight away, with no separate password. Email capitalization does not matter. The class is derived from the selected subteam. Chief Engineers have no membership.
3. Select **Change role** to change someone's role, team or subteam. The change applies the next time they load a page.
4. Select **Restrict access** to stop someone signing in while keeping them in the current list. **Restore access** lets them sign in again.
5. When someone leaves the club, select **Remove person**, review the confirmation, then select **Remove person** again. They disappear from **Current people** and cannot sign in. Their assignments, logged hours and task history remain with their name.
6. If they return, open **Removed people**, search for them and select **Restore person**. This restores the same account and sign-in access without losing their contributions. Do not add a duplicate account.
7. To remove them from both People lists permanently, choose **Permanently remove** in **Removed people**, type their full name exactly as shown, and confirm. This cannot be undone. Their email and sign-in credentials are erased, and they leave the roster; their name stays on their assignments, hours and history. If they rejoin, add a new account with their original email. Their old contributions remain with the original record. **Cancel** leaves the account restorable.

Both restriction and removal block actions from already-open pages. The last active Chief Engineer cannot be removed or restricted. Officers and members cannot manage People.

## Assign yourself to a task

1. Open a task and select **Edit task**, or start with **New Task**.
2. Choose the project. In **Assigned members**, search for your own name and select it. Keep any other members who should remain assigned.
3. Optionally choose yourself as **Primary contact**, then save the task.
4. Open **My Tasks** to find your work. You can update its status and record your hours on the task page; your hours also appear in reports.

Chief Engineers can assign themselves to any team's tasks without a subteam membership. An officer editing an already-assigned chief's task can keep that assignment. Promoting a member to Chief Engineer preserves their existing assignments; changing a chief to a member or officer requires resolving assignments outside the destination team.

## Messages and what to do

These are the messages only the Chief Engineer can meet.

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subteam is required for a member or officer | A membership is missing. | Choose Class · Subteam before saving. |
| Subteam must be blank for a Chief Engineer | An administrator cannot also have a roster membership. | Clear the membership. Existing assignments remain when promoting someone to Chief Engineer. |
| cannot change teams until incompatible active assignments are resolved | The person's active task assignments belong to their former class. | End or otherwise resolve those assignments before changing class. |
| Email has already been taken | Another person on the People list already uses that email. | Search Current people and Removed people. Edit or restore the existing account. |
| Email is invalid | The email address is incomplete. | Type the full address, for example `name@tamu.edu`. |
| At least one active Chief Engineer is required. Add another Chief Engineer before removing this role or access. | The change would leave nobody able to manage people. | Add or restore another Chief Engineer first, then repeat the change. |
| Subteam must belong to the person's team | The chosen subteam is in a different class. | Choose a Class · Subteam from the person's class. |
